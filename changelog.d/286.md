- **A secret Library command someone else changed waits until you've looked at it.** In the box that asks before it runs, **Run** stays greyed until you press **Show command**, and the box says who changed it and when ("Changed by bob@example.com on …", or "Changed on another device"). Once you've run it after seeing it, it runs as before until someone else changes its text again. Your own secret commands, and commands that aren't secret, work as before.
