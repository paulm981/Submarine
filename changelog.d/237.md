- **SSH tabs behind the same jump host can share one login to it.** Opening a folder of servers behind a jump host asks for its password or code once, not once per tab, and the jump host sees one login for up to 16 tabs. It's off until you turn on **One login per jump host** in Settings › Servers & sessions. A huge dump (a `cat` of a big file) through a shared login scrolls about half as fast, and a jump host that records sessions sees those tabs as one login. ([#237](https://github.com/paulm981/Submarine/issues/237))
